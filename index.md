## What is Magic City Savers?

Magic City Savers helps people who shop at more than one grocery store find the best weekly deals in one place.

We're starting in central Birmingham neighborhoods like Avondale, Crestwood, Forest Park, Homewood, and nearby areas. Right now, we review weekly sales across local stores and share:
